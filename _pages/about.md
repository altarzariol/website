I am currently an assistant professor at the Univeristy of Klagenfurt (Austria), in the department of Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity. \\
Furthermore, since November 2025 I am leading as Principal Investigator an FWF-founded project called <a href="https://www.fwf.ac.at/en/research-radar/10.55776/PAT1599524">EX3: EXplain and EXploit Knowledge
EXtracted to Improve ASP Performance</a>. 

My research interest is a subfield of Artificial Intelligence called Knowledge Representation
and Reasoning, particularly Answer Set Programming (ASP), a rule-based declarative
programming paradigm that has shown potential for modelling and solving challenging
industrial problems. More specifically, I pursued the topic of discovering and exploiting
symbolic knowledge to improve the efficiency of solving complex decision and optimisation
problems via ASP.
